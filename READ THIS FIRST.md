# Pastel-Cards-Free-No-Subscription-Quizlet-Alternative
How to download:
1. click the green button that says "<> Code"
2. click "Download ZIP"
3. go through the zip file and click "pastelcards"

It was made using Grok, Gemini, and Claude (using only their free tier). If you encounter any bugs, tell me.

Since I made it using A.I. without me writing any line of code, do not make closed-source version of this and make money off of it. it should be free for everyone to use. this is only available for laptops and PCs, it is an HTML file (unless you can make it work on your phone).

Features it has:
- make cards in a deck (can be deleted, edited, renamed)
- you can star cards and only study the cards you starred in any quiz mode
Quiz modes:
1. Flashcards (you can flip through the cards, skip the cards, or classify the cards as "hard, again, and ok")
2. Match Cards Game (just like quizlet's card game but it has the option to only have 6 cards or 12 cards to quiz you. there is no timer for this and you can use your mouse's scroll wheel to scroll through a long card to view the rest of it)
3. Asteroid Game (the pink circles are asteroids. there are two modes: one is "🌸 Chill Practice (No Lives)" and the other is "⚡Challenge Mode: ON (3 lives). you click the "🌸 Chill Practice (No Lives)" to turn the challenge mode on. there are also two speed settings where the asteroids can fall down the screen gently or normally.
- it has SRS, and in flashcards this is what the buttons mean: Hard = I didn't know it (comes back soon & often) · Again = show me once more · Ok = got it
- the SRS is not only available in the flashcard mode, but also the rest of the two modes.

Level	How a card gets there
🆕 New	(Never answered yet cuz the html file never quizzed you on it yet)
🌱 Familiar	(Answered, and right 1–3 times in a row)
🌸 Learned (Right 4 times in a row)
👑 Mastered	(Right again after resting a day, then again after a week)
